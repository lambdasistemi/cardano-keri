\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries From observations to freshness}\\[4pt]
{Research definitions for one chosen identity and history}
\end{center}

For a supplied finite event set $E$, let $s_e$ be an event's sequence,
$p_e$ its publication time, and $a_e$ its evidence-availability time.
Only events with both times known and already reached contribute:
\[
 E_t=\{e\in E\mid p_e\text{ and }a_e\text{ known},\ p_e\le t,\ a_e\le t\},
 \qquad F_E(t)=\max_{e\in E_t}s_e\quad(E_t\ne\varnothing).
\]
The empty set yields \emph{unknown}, not sequence zero.
If the chain projects sequence $c$, the supported observed lag is
\[
 L(c,F)=
 \begin{cases}
 F-c & c\le F,\\
 \text{ahead of observation }(c,F) & c>F.
 \end{cases}
\]
Missing projection, unknown observations and unsupported observations have
separate results. Example: $F=3$, $c=1$ gives $L=2$ events.
Zero observed lag does not rule out hidden events.

For one event with publication time $p_e$ and inclusion time $t_e$,
\[
 D(e,t_e)=t_e-p_e\qquad\text{when }p_e\text{ is known and }p_e\le t_e.
\]
Otherwise delay is undefined. Event counts and time are different units.
Consumer maturity is another property:
\[
 \mathrm{eligible}(s,t)\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad L(s,F_E(t))=0.
\]
The draft exhibits an eligible checkpoint three events behind its observation.

\textbf{Mirror obligation.} For registry $r$ and revocation identifier $x$,
coverage means that $r$ exists and its stored revoked set contains $x$.
Completeness quantifies over valid anchored revocation seal walks:
\[
 \text{valid anchored revocation walk for }x\text{ in }r
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{Covered}(r,x).
\]
This is a proposed coverage requirement, not a completeness result established
by the checkpoint metric. The draft proves no composition or independence of
the two models.

\textbf{Lean map:} \texttt{frontierFrom}, \texttt{sequenceLag},
\texttt{projectionDelay}, \texttt{eligible},\\
\texttt{RevocationCovered},
\texttt{MirrorComplete}. Observations and their authenticity/completeness
remain supplied research inputs.

\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Three conditional research claims}\\[4pt]
{All three Lean statements are intentionally unproved}
\end{center}

\textbf{Accounting definition.} For one agent and a fixed horizon,
\[
 U=R+P_{\rm returned}+P_{\rm terminal}
       -P_{\rm committed}-F_{\rm sponsor}-C.
\]
$C$ sums monitoring, success, failure, racing, locked-capital and manufacturing
costs. Principal returned against equal principal committed is not profit.
Units, costs, control of addresses and terminal valuation are research choices.

\textbf{C1: submit versus stop.} Restrict to a lawful keep rotation without
refund change, with both choices feasible. Its account has no capital input:
\[
 U_{\rm submit}=r-C,\qquad U_{\rm stop}=0,
 \qquad r>C\ \Longrightarrow\ U_{\rm submit}>U_{\rm stop}.
\]
$r$ is the actual addressed rotation premium. Example: $10-3=7$;
with costs $12$, utility is $-2$. This comparison proves neither equilibrium
nor timely submission. Lean: \texttt{C1\_submit\_over\_stop}.

\textbf{C2: compose separate delay bounds.} For a lawful included event with
ordered publication, observation, submission and inclusion times,
\[
 t_o\le t_p+\delta_o,\quad
 t_s\le t_o+\delta_s,\quad
 t_i\le t_s+\delta_i
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 t_i-t_p\le\delta_o+\delta_s+\delta_i.
\]
Each bound needs its own justification. Inclusion is already a premise;
this is not a liveness guarantee. Lean: \texttt{C2\_delay\_composition}.

\textbf{C3: finite funded service.} For an actual trace containing only
rotations and pool top-ups, fixed premium $P>0$, initial pool $Q_0$,
total added funding $T$, and budget $B$,
\[
 Q_0+T\le B
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \sum_j r_j\le B\quad\land\quad N_{\rm paid}P\le B.
\]
This bounds premium spending and paid rotations, not all costs or unpaid
progress. Example: $Q_0=20$, $P=10$, $T=0$ supports two paid updates.
Lean: \texttt{C3\_funded\_work\_bound}.

\textbf{Discussion target.} Choose the service metric, justify observations
and costs, model competition and funding renewal, then strengthen or replace
these statements. The Lean types are authoritative for their exact premises;
these formulas are their reading guide.
