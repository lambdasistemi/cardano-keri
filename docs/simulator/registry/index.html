The registry UTxO

A fold spends this UTxO at its current generation; a stale generation is the transaction failing on a spent input, at no cost. Requests, retracts and convictions never touch it.

What this page is

  • A JavaScript transcription of stepFn and replay from lean/CardanoKeri/Registry.lean, embedded byte-for-byte from simulator/registry-simulator-core.mjs. The fifteen stories and the Lean corpus below are embedded too; ?selftest=1 replays all of it through this page's own core.
  • The embedded corpus is emitted by lean/RegistryTraceDriver.lean from stepFn itself via ToJson; registry-simulator-trace-gate.mjs re-runs Lean, compares by sha256 and replays every cell here: .
  • Modelled: requests as inbox UTxOs; a permissionless fold at a named generation with process and reject; retract in phase 2; close deleting the row with the burn; convict keeping the row with the tombstone; the plugin pinned; value as bonds locked or refunded, tips per request. Not modelled: cryptography (evidence is a table), the checkpoint's own life (rotations, poison — that is the checkpoint simulator), UTxO mechanics beyond the registry's generation, the ledger's fee.
