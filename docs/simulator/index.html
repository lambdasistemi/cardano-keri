  • The machine on this page is one transcription of CardanoKeri.Checkpoint.stepFn: same actions, same guards in the same order, same flows, same refusals — and where Lean returns none, this page names the reason. The page embeds the core byte-for-byte (checkpoint-simulator-build.mjs); checkpoint-simulator-scenario-gate.mjs goes red when the embedded copy drifts.
  • The embedded corpus below is emitted by lean/CheckpointTraceDriver.lean from stepFn itself, via ToJson. checkpoint-simulator-trace-gate.mjs re-runs Lean, compares by sha256, and replays every step through this page's JavaScript: stated sha256 shown at load.
  • Modelling assumptions (from Checkpoint.lean, D-034): a freeze is not enabled from a poisoned state; a conviction sends the freeze bond and the pool to the refund address while the conviction bond goes to the convictor.
  • Consumability here is the state-side conjuncts only: both bonds full, not poisoned, past juvenility. The consumer's own threshold signature on its payment — and validity (D-027, A11), reserved in the datum — are outside this machine.
