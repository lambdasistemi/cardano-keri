What this is and is not

This is an executable transcription of Registry.stepFn and the pinned public Singular transaction judgments. The registry holds absent, active or permanently retired keys. Carriers retain their token and key while parked. Conviction preserves custody pending booking; only a consuming fold retires the key and burns its token.
