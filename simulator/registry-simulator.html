The registry

one UTxO holding a leaf per AID — active, dormant, convicted — checkpoints that come and go, requests that never contend, folds that race, reaps that pay for themselves — played against the machine of Registry.lean slot 0

The registry UTxO

A fold spends this UTxO at its current generation; a stale generation is the transaction failing on a spent input, at no cost. Requests, retracts, reaps, pauses, resumes and checkpoint convictions never touch it. A leaf, once inserted, is permanent.

Checkpoints

The inbox

Phase 1: a fold may process. Phase 2: only the owner may retract. Phase 3 (or a future timestamp): anyone may reject a posted request. A go-request is dated at the end of time: never phase 2, never rejectable — only processed.

What this page is

  • A JavaScript transcription of stepFn and replay from lean/CardanoKeri/Registry.lean, embedded byte-for-byte from simulator/registry-simulator-core.mjs. The fifteen stories and the Lean corpus below are embedded too; ?selftest=1 replays all of it through this page's own core.
  • The embedded corpus is emitted by lean/RegistryTraceDriver.lean from stepFn itself via ToJson; registry-simulator-trace-gate.mjs re-runs Lean, compares by sha256 and replays every cell here: .
  • Modelled: leaves active(token) / dormant(k) / convicted; checkpoints live / parked / tombstone; requests as inbox UTxOs for registration, revival and conviction of a dormant AID; go-requests created only by a reap, dated at the end of time; a permissionless fold at a named generation with process and reject; retract in phase 2; pause and resume by the next keys; conviction of a live or parked checkpoint by a proof; the reap after the grace window (or by the owner, or at once for a tombstone) and its value split; the plugin pinned. Not modelled: cryptography (evidence is a table), rotations that keep a checkpoint live, the checkpoint's bonds beyond one abstract D, the ledger's fee (the samaritan theorems carry it as a parameter).
